### Method notes (what this example teaches)
- The pypdf rendering IS the source: "OJice Hours" (ff -> J), ﬁ/ﬂ ligatures
  ("certiﬁcation", "ﬁle format") stay exactly as garbled in every verbatim field.
  Extract THROUGH the noise; never "repair" it.
- This syllabus actually prints instructor contact and office hours (rare — 3 of
  6,856 Spring-2026 syllabi print office hours): both captured verbatim, garbling
  included.
- The textbook contradiction: the bullet label says "Required Textbook", the book's
  own detail block says "THIS TEXT IS NOT REQUIRED, BUT IS RECOMMENDED!". The
  specific statement overrides the generic label -> required: false, raw_text spans
  BOTH printed signals (joined with a bracketed elision), the contradiction is
  recorded in distinctive_features, and confidence is medium — a resolved ambiguity,
  visibly resolved.
- inclusive_access stays null: the IncludED passage is conditional boilerplate ("If
  your Dallas College course requires learning materials, then..."), the only named
  text is not required, and the real materials (camera ~$700, Adobe, tripod) are
  bought from outside retailers. true would overclaim; the tension is recorded
  instead.
- Points sum exactly to the printed "TOTAL: 100 points" checksum (excluded from
  grading[]); every count comes from printed "N @ M points each" notation.
- "NO AI (Artiﬁcial Intelligence) is allowed in the creation/production of your
  photographs." -> policies.ai_plagiarism, garbled ligature and all.
