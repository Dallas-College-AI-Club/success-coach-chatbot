Course Description

Study of the concepts and principles necessary to perform intermediate or advanced nursing skills for the adult patient and demonstrate competence in the performance of nursing procedures. Content includes knowledge, judgment, skills and professional values within a legal/ethical framework. This course lends itself to a blocked approach.

Instructor-Defined Learning Outcomes

  1. Apply concepts and principles necessary for the performance of nursing skills for the adult patient and demonstrate competence/clinical reasoning in the performance of selected nursing procedures and practices. (SLO 1)
  2. Identify the five rights of medication administration, using the doctor’s orders and medication administration record. (SLO 3)
  3. Communicate clearly the English language in speaking, reading, and writing during performance and evaluation of nursing procedures/simulated nurse-patient interactions. (SLO 5)
  4. Analyze clinical situations which require application of problem solving and the nursing process during skills performance and simulation. (SLO 1)
  5. Incorporate safety aspects of all advanced nursing skills. (SLO 4 & 5)
  6. Utilize contemporary health related equipment and demonstrate its safe use. (SLO 4 & 5)
  7. Demonstrate nursing skills consistent with evidenced based practice. (SLO 4)
  8. Interpret the scientific and ethical principles as it relates to each nursing skill performed. (SLO 1)
  9. Demonstrate efficient and effective use of resources in the lab or simulated clinical setting. (SLO 4)
  10. Implement measures to promote quality and a safe environment for self and others. (SLO 4)
  11. Obtain instruction, supervision, or training as needed when implementing nursing procedures or practices. (SLO 3 & 4)
  12. Communicate and manage information using technology to support decision-making to improve patient care. (SLO 2)
  13. Use clinical reasoning and knowledge based on evidence-based practice outcomes as a basis for decision making. (SLO 1)

Students will be notified via email of any unexpected class changes. Class is automatically canceled any day Dallas College cancels class or closes campus. Students will be notified via email with contingency plans to cover course material should there be any class cancelation or campus closure. 

Attendance and Participation

Instructor and Nursing Program Attendance Policies

Students are expected to attend all classes. Students are responsible for attending class and consulting with the instructor when an absence occurs. If for some reason you must leave class early, you should inform the instructor prior to the start of class of your reason for leaving early.

Students must begin attendance in all classes of enrollment on the first day of class. No exceptions. Financial Aid will not be granted to students who have been certified as not attending by the certification date. For this class, your physical participation in class, on or before the certification date will allow you to receive credit for FA purposes. For certification dates, check with the division or FAO for further information. Students who are not certified as beginning class are responsible for any payments due as a result of non-certification, including dropping out of the course.

Only the course instructor, in conjunction with the Department Chair, can excuse a student from class or course responsibilities. In case of inclement weather, the Dallas College inclement weather policy will be followed. In the event of an illness, accident, or emergency, the student should make direct contact with the instructor before a class, presentation, examination, or other activity takes place. If the instructor cannot be reached in person or by telephone, the student should leave a message with the nursing program administrative assistant or the instructor via voice mail. An extenuating circumstance will be handled by the instructor, with the Department Chair. Drop action may be taken by the instructor if a student is absent more than ten percent (10%) of total class hours.

Policy Description:  Students are expected to attend all classes (clinical, class, and skills lab) and scheduled activities in order to meet course objectives. Attendance will be monitored by the instructor. Students must consult with the instructor when an absence occurs. The Instructor will initiate drop action if a student is absent more than ten percent (10%) of total lecture class hours.

Absence policy

There are no provisions for excused absences other than the following:

  1. A copy of the subpoena must be presented.
  2. Death of immediate family members. A copy of the obituary or funeral program must be presented to the instructor. (Parents, Grandparents, Siblings, Children or at the discretion of the Department Chair)
  3. Religious holidays. Student must provide a written statement including:
  4. Date of the holiday and the reason why class attendance is impossible.
  5. An alternative assignment will be given and must be completed in a timely manner.
  6. Military obligations. A copy of the orders must be presented.
  7. Emergency situation deemed appropriate by the Department Chair. (ex. Student in Emergency Room, car accident)

If an excused absence occurs in the class or lab, the student is responsible to obtain class notes and assignments. 

Tardiness

Tardiness is an unprofessional behavior, distracts other students, and reduces all students’ ability to learn.  In non-clinical nursing courses (lecture and skills), tardiness is considered unprofessional behavior and unacceptable. The classroom door will be locked at 10 minutes after class start time and the student will not be permitted to enter class until the next break time (Faculty Discretion). 

In most situations, the student will receive a written warning the first time. The second time, a written final warning will be completed, signed by the student, and placed in the student's file. The third time, the student will be dismissed from the program. 

NOTE: If for some reason the student must leave class early, the student should inform the instructor before the start of class with the reason for leaving early.

Late Work/Make Up Policy

Late work will not be accepted.  Any work turned in late or incomplete will receive a 0% grade.

There are no make-up exams. If a student is absent during the assigned time for an exam, the student will receive a 0% and will forfeit one skill check-off exam attempt. Competency Validation Policy

Competency Validation Policy

The grading of the skills validations are as follows:

If a competency validation is passed on the first attempt, the grade will be 100%. If passed on the second attempt, the grade will be 90% or a B. If passed on the third attempt, the grade will be 80% or a C. If the competency validation is not passed on the third attempt, the student will receive a grade of 0% for that skill. If the student fails to complete a competency validation successfully in 3 attempts, the student will not be able to attend hospital clinicals and will be withdrawn from the nursing program.

Before competency validation for each skill, all students must complete 1-hour of practice, outside of class during open lab hours.  If the student does not pass the competency validation on the first attempt, they must spend a minimum of 1-hour practicing the skill not passed before they are allowed to complete their second attempt. A time to complete the second attempt must be made with the instructor to complete the second attempt at the competency validation. If the student arrives for the second attempt without completing the minimum 1-hour practice time in the nursing skills lab, they will forfeit their second attempt. If a student does not pass the competency validation on the second attempt, the student must make an appointment with the nursing skills lab Instructional Specialist/Clinical Professor/Student Mentor for one-on-one tutoring in the skill. The student will be eligible for their third attempt only after meeting with the Instructional Specialist/Clinical Professor/Student Mentor and making an appointment to complete the third attempt.

The forms for the skill competency validations will be posted on Brightspace. Students should print them and bring them to the assigned class. 

Faculty will determine the specific assignments and evaluations for their courses. The focus of this course is nursing skills and the related theory base for their use. Students will learn a wide variety of skills although there will be only 6 competency validation experiences as listed above. The student will have three chances to pass each competency validation. Students will be required to view assigned videos and complete other assignments prior to class. The content will be imbedded in the assignments, written work, in-class performance, and simulation. There may be quizzes and other learning activities at the instructor’s discretion.

Grade Appeals:

A student who wishes to appeal a grade should follow these guidelines:

  1. Make an appointment immediately with your instructor to discuss your performance and the grade you received.
  2. If you are unable to resolve the situation at that level, make an appointment to speak with the Nursing Department Chair
  3. If you are unsatisfied, you may request an appointment with the Executive Dean of Nursing.
  4. If you are still dissatisfied, you may request an appointment with the Dallas College Vice Provost of the School of Health Sciences.
  5. Students may also discuss the situation with the Dallas College Mountain View counselors, but this does not take the place of formal appeals via the chain of command as listed above.
