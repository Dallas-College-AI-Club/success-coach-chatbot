# Dallas College - Course Syllabus
## HIST 1301: History of the United States I (3 Credit Hours)
### Course Information
* **Course ID**: HIST-1301-22005
* **Term**: Fall 2026
* **Class Location**: Liberal Arts Building, Room 104

### Instructor Information
* **Instructor**: Prof. John Smith
* **Email**: jsmith@dallascollege.edu
* **Office Hours**: Tuesday & Thursday, 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

### Course Description
A survey of the social, political, economic, cultural, and intellectual history of the United States from the pre-Columbian era to the Civil War/Reconstruction period.

### Required Materials
* **Textbook**: OpenStax U.S. History. 
* **Cost**: This is an Open Educational Resource (OER) textbook. Students can download the textbook for free as a PDF online at the OpenStax website or read it online. **No physical textbook purchase is required.**
* **Secondary Readings**: Articles and source documents will be posted weekly on eCampus.

### Grading Scale & Policy
Grades will be calculated as follows:
* Weekly Quizzes (10 total, lowest dropped): 50% of final grade
* Historiography Essay (1 total, due Week 8): 20% of final grade
* Final Examination: 30% of final grade

Grading Rubric:
* A: 90 - 100%
* B: 80 - 89%
* C: 70 - 79%
* D: 60 - 69%
* F: Below 60%

### Historiography Essay Details
Students are required to write exactly **1 essay** (1,500 - 2,000 words) analyzing a primary document of their choice from the pre-Civil War era. This constitutes the entirety of the essay requirement for this course.

### Course Schedule
* Week 1: Pre-Columbian Societies and Transatlantic Encounters
* Week 2: Colonial America and the Atlantic World
* Week 3: Causes of the American Revolution
* Week 4: The Revolutionary War and the Constitution
* Week 5: The Early Republic & Jacksonian Democracy (Midterm Quiz)
* Week 6: Industrialization, Market Revolution, and Westward Expansion
* Week 7: Sectional Conflict and the Compromise of 1850
* Week 8: The Civil War (Essay Submission due by Friday)
* Week 9: Reconstruction and its Aftermath
* Week 10: Final Course Exam
