Instructor-Defined Learning Outcomes

Dr. Seaton has realigned the learning outcomes to make sure they reflect today’s expectations for workforce‑ready skills.

BCIS-1305/1405 Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

  1. Explain how the core components of information systems work together to support everyday business activities.
  2. Describe common business technologies and applications and explain how organizations use them to improve communication and productivity.
  3. Explain how data, networks, and communication tools create a connected world and apply basic networking ideas to simple real‑world examples.
  4. Describe the purpose of business intelligence and decision‑support tools and interpret basic data outputs such as charts or dashboards.
  5. Explain common information security risks and apply basic protective practices to safeguard personal and organizational information.
  6. Describe how organizations use enterprise systems and e‑commerce tools to manage operations and customer interactions.
  7. Explain the role of artificial intelligence and emerging technologies in modern business environments and identify practical examples of how they are used today.
  8. Identify organizational and global factors that influence technology adoption and explain how these factors shape technology decisions.
