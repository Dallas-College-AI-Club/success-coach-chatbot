#  **4\. Sean — The Urgent International Student**

### **Persona Type:** International Student  **Age:** Around 22  **Scope Status:** **MVP Primary Target**

### **A. Name & Archetype**

### Sean is an international student who needs very fast and safe help when he loses important documents, like a passport or ID.

### **B. Background**

### Because he is in a new country, these documents are very important for his identity, school access, and legal status. Losing them is not just an inconvenience — it is a serious problem.

### When this happens, Sean feels strong stress and urgency. He cannot wait or figure things out slowly. He needs clear instructions right away.

### **C. Core Goals & Frustrations**

### **Goals**

* ### **Get back important documents as soon as possible**    Sean wants to find his documents quickly. These items are very important for his school and daily life, so he cannot wait too long.

* ### **Know exactly where to go and who to contact**    Sean does not know the system well. He needs clear directions, like which office to visit or who to talk to for help.

* ### **Get clear and safe instructions**    Sean wants simple and correct guidance. He does not want confusing information or advice that could cause problems.

### **Frustrations:**

* ### **Losing important documents causes high stress**    Sean feels very worried when he loses documents like his passport or ID. These are very important for school and daily life.

* ### **Not sure who to contact first**    Sean is confused about what to do. He is not sure if he should contact a campus office, security, or International Student Services. This makes the situation harder for him.

* ### **Wrong or unclear information can make the situation worse**    Sean is afraid of getting the wrong advice. If the information is not clear, he might take the wrong steps, which could cause bigger problems.

### **D. MVP Matrix** 

| Feature | How Sean Uses It |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Lost & Found** | Searches for lost documents and gets urgent help |

### **E. Needs from the Chatbot** 

* ### **Detect important items (passport, ID) as urgent**     When Sean reports a lost passport or ID, the chatbot should recognize that this is urgent. It should give fast help and clear priority support.

* ### **Search Lost & Found records quickly**     Sean needs fast results. The chatbot should quickly check if someone has found his document.

* ### **Give clear steps if the item is found**    If the item is found, Sean needs simple instructions. For example, where to go and how to get it back.

* ### **Provide International Student Services contact information**    Sean needs official help. The chatbot should give contact details for International Student Services or other support offices.

* ### **Give safe guidance without legal advice**    The chatbot should give general help only. It should not give legal or immigration advice. It should guide Sean to the right office for that.
