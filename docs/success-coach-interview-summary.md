# Success Coach Interview Summary

## Interview Information

**Interview Method:** Email  
**Interviewee:** Jasmine Suggs, Dallas College Success Coach  
**Purpose:** To understand what information, documents, questions, and contextual details students should prepare before meeting with a Success Coach for degree planning support.

## Student Preparation Items

Students should come prepared with:

- Student ID number
- A general idea of career or educational goals, if known
- A list of questions they want answered
- Knowledge of any colleges previously attended
- Information about military service, AP, CLEP, IB, or dual credit
- Any transfer transcripts that have not yet been evaluated
- Work schedule and availability for classes
- Personal responsibilities that could affect scheduling, such as work, children, or transportation

## Documents, Systems, and Student Records to Review

Students should review:

- Academic Progress
- Academic Plan, if one already exists
- Current class schedule
- Unofficial transcript
- Placement test results, if applicable
- Holds on their account
- Financial aid status
- Registration eligibility
- Transfer credit evaluation, if applicable

Students should also be able to access:

- Workday
- Student email
- Navigate

## Common Information Gaps and Pain Points

Common gaps include:

- Not knowing which degree they want
- Confusing certificates with associate degrees
- Assuming every class counts toward their program
- Not understanding prerequisites
- Not realizing transfer credits must be officially evaluated
- Thinking financial aid pays for unlimited classes
- Not checking for registration holds
- Not knowing graduation requirements
- Believing they can take courses in any order
- Being unaware of GPA requirements for their program

## Questions Students Should Be Prepared to Ask

Students should be prepared to ask:

- Am I in the correct program of study?
- What classes should I take next semester?
- How long will it take to graduate?
- Will my transfer credits count?
- Am I eligible to graduate?
- What happens if I change my major?
- Can I attend part-time?
- How will dropping a class affect me?
- Which courses are only offered during certain semesters?
- What should I complete before transferring to a university?
- How can I improve my academic standing?
- What campus resources should I use?

## Helpful Student Goals and Background Information

Helpful information includes:

### Academic Goals

- Degree or certificate sought
- Intended transfer university
- Career interests
- Graduation timeline

### Personal Factors

- Employment status
- Family responsibilities
- Preferred learning format, such as online, in-person, or hybrid
- Transportation availability
- Financial concerns

### Academic History

- Previous colleges attended
- Credits earned
- Current GPA
- Developmental education needs
- Academic probation or suspension history

## Common Misunderstandings

| Misunderstanding | Reality |
|---|---|
| All college credits transfer. | Transferability depends on the receiving institution and program. |
| Every course counts toward my degree. | Only courses listed in the degree plan fulfill program requirements. |
| Changing majors does not affect graduation. | A major change can add time and additional coursework. |
| Transfer credits automatically appear. | Official transcripts must be submitted and evaluated. |
| Advising is only needed before registration. | Advising supports academic planning, career exploration, and staying on track throughout college. |
| Passing a class means it satisfies my degree requirement. | A passing grade does not necessarily fulfill a specific program requirement. |
| Financial aid covers any number of classes. | Aid eligibility depends on enrollment status, satisfactory academic progress, and program requirements. |

## Chatbot Recommendations

The chatbot could ask students:

- What degree or certificate are you pursuing?
- Have you declared your major?
- Are you planning to transfer?
- Do you know which university you hope to attend?
- Have you attended another college?
- Have all official transcripts been submitted to Admissions?
- Do you have military, AP, CLEP, IB, or dual-credit coursework?
- What semester are you planning for?
- Are you planning to attend full-time or part-time?
- Do you prefer online, hybrid, or in-person classes?
- Have you reviewed your Academic Progress report on Workday?
- Have you checked for holds on your Workday account?
- Can you access your Workday account and school email?
- Do you have a list of questions for your assigned Success Coach?
- What are your career goals?
- When would you like to graduate?
- Are there any challenges that may affect your schedule, such as work, childcare, or transportation?

## Suggested Chatbot Pre-Appointment Checklist

Before a Success Coach appointment, the chatbot should remind students to:

- Review Academic Progress and Academic Plan.
- Check student email for important updates.
- Log in to Workday to review any registration holds.
- Review current schedule and completed coursework.
- Submit any outstanding official transcripts to Admissions.
- Write down academic and career goals through the Success Plan.
- Make a list of questions for the assigned Success Coach.
- Be prepared to discuss work schedule, family responsibilities, transportation, and preferred class format.

## Chatbot Support Requirements

The chatbot should:

1. Collect basic academic planning information before the appointment.
2. Ask whether the student has previous college, military, AP, CLEP, IB, or dual-credit coursework.
3. Remind students to review Workday, student email, Navigate, Academic Progress, holds, and financial aid status.
4. Help students identify questions to bring to their Success Coach.
5. Explain common misunderstandings about degree plans, transfer credits, prerequisites, and financial aid.
6. Generate a personalized pre-appointment checklist.
7. Encourage students to consider scheduling barriers such as work, family responsibilities, transportation, and preferred course format.
8. Avoid making promises about transferability, financial aid eligibility, graduation approval, or degree completion timelines without Success Coach confirmation.

## Conclusion

This interview confirmed that students benefit from structured preparation before meeting with a Success Coach. A chatbot can improve the advising experience by helping students gather key information, review important systems, identify questions, and understand common degree planning issues before their appointment.
